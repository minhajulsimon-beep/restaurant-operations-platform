Revenue and payments are separate

Net sales exclude sales tax and tips. Financial refunds reduce sales on their posting business date; they do not return food to stock. Collection checks include tax and tips. These are POS ledger controls, not processor-settlement or bank reconciliation.

Operational contribution, not net profit

Food usage is opening stock + receipts − closing stock, valued at fixed standard cost. Contribution deducts that usage and labor from net sales. It excludes rent, utilities, depreciation, processor fees, and other overhead.

Demonstration data and local refresh

All restaurant names, transactions, and workforce records are fictional. The pipeline intentionally includes errors to show detection. This report is a snapshot: rerun the pipeline and reopen the file to see refreshed data.
